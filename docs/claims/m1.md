**Note**: The AUC values are currently quite low (~0.55-0.61), indicating low signal in the generated dataset. We are relying on the generator's current parameter distributions.

**Sensitivity grid summary**: Under our documented assumptions, the reject-inference model delivers a positive lift (extra approvals at equal loss) in all 9 cells of the kappa × bias_strength grid. The lift is largest when defaults are timing-driven (high kappa) and bureau-bias is strong — exactly the setting where our alt-data model has the most advantage over the bureau-dependent legacy policy. The lift shrinks when defaults are solvency-dominated (low kappa) and bias is weak, because solvency defaults are harder to predict with any model, and weak bias means the legacy policy already approves most creditworthy firms. Thin-file AUC lift is mixed: it is positive in some cells but negative in others, reflecting the difficulty of scoring thin-file borrowers even with reject inference. These results are method validation, not India-level forecasts.
